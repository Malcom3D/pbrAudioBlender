# pbrAudioBlender
Welcome to release **v0.3.0** of **pbrAudioBlender**! This major update brings a complete audio-visual workflow suite directly into Blender, combining advanced acoustic simulation, rendering, and DAW integration.

* **New 3D Viewport & GUI Tools:** Enjoy brand-new viewport panels, 3D viewport widgets, and visual ray previews (`viewRay`) to inspect acoustic paths right inside your scene.
* **Flexible Acoustic Rendering:** Choose from multiple output formats for your acoustic renderings, including Ambisonic, Surround, Stereo, ProLogic II, and Binaural (Stereo with HRTF processing).
* **Advanced Material & Frequency Nodes:** Utilize preset acoustic materials and an expansive set of new node types (such as spatial frequency responses, response curves, charts, and preview nodes) for precise sound tuning.
* **Reliable Exporters & Storage:** Improved collision and render data exporters now feature automatic mesh validation and fixing, alongside optimized data storage and memory management.
* **Ecosystem Integration:** Seamlessly connect with `pbrAudioRender`, `pbrAudioShaders`, and `pbrAudioDAW` (complete with a new TUI) for a unified physical-based audio pipeline.
